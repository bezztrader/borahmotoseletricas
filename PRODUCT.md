# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS + Motion + Lenis, pinned by the brief. Mobile-first.

## Users

People in Jacarepaguá / Freguesia (Rio de Janeiro), mostly arriving from Instagram on a phone, considering an electric moto, scooter or bike to escape traffic and fuel costs. The job: understand why electric, see the models, and book a test ride on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

One-page site for Borah Motos Elétricas, Freguesia unit: presents the store, models and services and leads to a WhatsApp test-ride booking. No prices on the site.

## Positioning

"E aí, Borah de elétrica?" — sale, test ride, in-store workshop and real after-sales, from a brand that started in Duque de Caxias and opened in Freguesia in 2026. Store signage: "MOBILIDADE URBANA INTELIGENTE".

## Operating Context

- WhatsApp (21) 98049-7952; Instagram @boraheletricajpa.
- Estrada dos Três Rios, 670 - Freguesia, Jacarepaguá, Rio de Janeiro - RJ. Mon–Sat 10h–19h.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- PT-BR copy, young/direct/carioca tone; no marketing clichés.
- No product prices anywhere. The savings calculator uses editable estimates in src/lib/economia.ts (fuel and energy only).
- Models: X13 (1000W motor, store favourite), T Max, Borah Max; specs are placeholders in src/lib/site.ts.
- Services: sale, maintenance/technical assistance in store, after-sales, tyre armour with Blin Max.

## Brand Commitments

- Symbol: battery/chip capsule with a bolt chip and side bars, yellow in an amber ring on black (redrawn in SVG: public/brand/). Logotype "B" + symbol + "rah", "MOTOS ELÉTRICAS" tracked below.
- Colours: Borah yellow #FFCC00 (measured #FFC702–#FFDB01 on the logo), amber #E6A800, black #0B0B0B, graphite #1A1A1A, light #F2F2F2. No purple, neon blue, lime or generic gradients.
- Type: Unbounded (display), Lexend (logotype), Manrope (body). No Inter.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo reference (with seasonal Santa hat, ignored): public/images/logo-referencia.jpg.
- Real store/model photos exist as Instagram covers in the owner's Downloads (borah-*.jpg, 360×640); the site does not use them yet (brief asked for illustration + generated mood visuals). They can fill the model photo slots.
- Mood visuals generated on Higgsfield (FLUX.2, illustrative, no brand): public/media/. The hero loop is built from generated stills with a camera move (the free plan cannot generate video).
- No testimonials, reviews or prices supplied. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Every path ends at a WhatsApp test ride.
2. Phone first; fast on 4G.
3. Show, then claim: the line-drawn X13, the savings maths, the store's real services.
4. Generated imagery is illustrative only and never presented as a specific model or the store.
